Mochi Mallow: Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Developer: Richard Owen.

Mochi Mallow is a match-3 game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account, sign-in or purchases. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: scores, coins, gems, skills, looks you own, settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, language) and daily goal progress. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your choice through Google's consent message before ads load.
  • You can change that choice at any time in the game: Settings > Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play.

Children

Mochi Mallow is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and you have a concern, contact us and we will help.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers or analytics that receive data from the game.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
